### Research Interests

My research focuses on **learning, adapting, and improving the capabilities of foundation models**. I am interested in understanding how knowledge transfers across domains and tasks, how models acquire new capabilities without erasing existing ones, and how post-training can improve the reasoning and decision-making abilities of large models and agents.

### Current Focus

- **LLM Post-training & Distillation** — capability integration, on-policy learning, and knowledge retention.
- **Multimodal Intelligence** — visual evidence grounding and reliable reasoning in large vision-language models.
- **Agent Learning & Reinforcement Learning** — improving long-horizon problem solving through post-training and verifier-guided optimization.
